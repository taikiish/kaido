Your drives

What Kaido stores

  • Your drives: GPS routes, times, distances and the places you passed through.
  • Your cars: make, model, year, color, photos, odometer readings and notes you add.
  • Settings and the milestones you earn.

All of this is saved on your iPhone and synced through your private iCloud account with Apple CloudKit. The developer can't see it.

Sensors

Location and motion

Kaido uses your location, including in the background, to record drives automatically, and motion data to notice when you start driving. Location is only used to build your own drive history and map.

Sensors

Camera and photos

The camera scans your VIN and reads your odometer, on your iPhone. Photos you pick are used for your car and profile pictures.

Optional

Friends and rankings

If you join Friends, Kaido saves a small profile to Kaido's shared CloudKit database so friends can compare stats. It contains:

  • The name and photo you choose, your country and your main car.
  • Totals such as miles this week, this month and overall, new roads, new counties and your longest trip.
  • Your invite code and the friend requests you send.

Your routes, exact locations and car photos are never published. Remove everything at any time with Profile › Delete my Friends profile.

Network

Services Kaido contacts

  • NHTSA vPIC (U.S. Department of Transportation): when you scan or type a VIN, the VIN is sent to look up your car's make and model.
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass API): to light up the streets you've driven, Kaido downloads street data for the areas around your drives.
  • geoBoundaries (on GitHub): Kaido downloads public region outlines for the countries you drive in.

These requests carry no account or personal details. Like any web request, they show your device's IP address to that service.

Your choices

Deleting your data

Delete drives and cars inside the app. To remove everything, delete the app, then remove Kaido's data in Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage. Your Friends profile can be deleted in the app, or email us and we'll remove it.

Age

Children

Kaido is made for licensed drivers and is not directed at children under 13.
