FAQ

Common questions

A drive didn't record

Set location access to Always and turn on Motion & Fitness for Kaido in iOS Settings. Kaido can't record after you swipe it away in the app switcher, so leave it open in the background.

Does Kaido drain my battery?

Kaido uses motion to wake up only when you start driving, then records GPS until you park. Most of the battery use happens during drives.

Who can see my drives?

Only you. Routes stay on your iPhone and in your iCloud. Friends only see the totals you share. Read the privacy policy.

How do I delete my Friends profile?

Open Profile › Delete my Friends profile. It removes your name, photo, totals and invite code. Your drives stay on your phone.

Can I try Kaido without driving?

Yes. In Settings, tap Load the demo garage or Load the Tokyo demo.
